# Survey and newspaper evidence for the SIH26072 proposal

Verified as of **30 September 2026**. This supplement separates empirical surveys, journalism, official corroboration, and design interpretation. It adds to [PROBLEM_EVIDENCE.md](PROBLEM_EVIDENCE.md). No interviews or field surveys were conducted for this project; the studies below are published third-party research.

## 1. Empirical surveys that can be cited

### S1 — IMD intermediate users: warning communication and response

- **Authors/date/type:** S. C. Bhan, Priyanka Singh, Lata Vishnoi and Shashank Yadubhushanam; January 2022; IMD meteorological monograph, *Expectation and Utilization Behaviour of the Intermediate Users of Weather and Climate Services in India*.
- **Primary source:** [Author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/359699450_Expectation_and_Utilization_Behaviour_of_the_IntermediateUsers_of_Weather_and_Climate_Services_in_India), printed p. 8 methods; p. 9 respondent profile; pp. 19–20, Figure 13 response barriers.
- **Scope:** Google Form circulated through IMD offices; responses collected **5 January–28 February 2021**. **515 respondents: 371 government and 144 nongovernment**; intermediate users, disaster managers and media, rather than a household probability sample.
- **Finding:** The report identifies **50%** citing inadequate communication of forecast uncertainty, **43%** citing insufficient awareness of safety actions, and **40%** citing missing impact information as barriers to appropriate response.
- **Limits:** These are respondents’ perceptions, not directly observed failures or percentages of Indian citizens. The barrier figure does not separately state valid item-response counts; do not convert rounded percentages into exact people. Historical online responses cannot establish current app effectiveness.
- **Evidence strength:** Primary technical report.

### S2 — NCAER: weather advisories used in livelihood decisions

- **Author/date/type:** National Council of Applied Economic Research; July 2020; MoES-commissioned report, *Estimating the Economic Benefits of Investment in Monsoon Mission and High Performance Computing Facilities*.
- **Primary source:** [IMD-hosted original report](https://rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in/uploads/survey/NCAER2020.pdf), printed pp. 15–17 sampling/profile, pp. 19–21 agricultural practices; PDF pages 39–41 and 43–45 respectively.
- **Scope:** NCAER–RFIS Survey 2019: **6,098 face-to-face beneficiaries**, comprising **3,965 farmers, 1,376 livestock owners and 757 marine fishermen**. Farmer respondents covered **121 districts in 11 states**. The report’s April 2015–March 2019 period concerns recalled data, not verified interview dates.
- **Finding:** **98% of surveyed farmers** reported changing at least one of nine agricultural practices using weather advisories.
- **Limits:** Sampling selected engaged voice-message listeners who also sought clarification from an existing service database. Self-reported behavior is not a national adoption rate or causal evaluation. Its separate approximately 200,000-person IVRS exercise must not be merged into the face-to-face denominator. No reported economic return should be transferred to the proposed app.
- **Evidence strength:** Primary commissioned evaluation; selection and recall limits.

### S3 — Nagaland lightning knowledge: a small historical pilot

- **Authors/date/type:** Alexander G. Keul, Sanjay Sharma and Luci Hidalgo Nunes; 3–7 June 2013 ECSS conference; *Can Lightning Really Hit You? Risk Preparedness Surveys in India, Brazil, Germany and Austria*.
- **Primary source:** [Original ESSL conference paper](https://www.essl.org/ECSS/2013/programme/abstracts/88.pdf), pp. 1–2 methods/Tables I and IV; p. 3 interpretation. Original PDF was retrieved directly and parsed when browser extraction failed.
- **Scope:** A **2012 pilot**, with **100 respondents in Nagaland**, within a multi-country study. Indian quota sample: 50% male, 50% female, mean age 35.8; 66% classified as highly educated.
- **Finding:** Table IV reports **3.0% correct** on a lightning-distance estimation item and **29.4% correct** on the proposition that a lightning strike necessarily kills instantly. Item-valid denominators are not separately supplied; preserve percentages as reported rather than infer counts.
- **Limits:** Small, nonprobability, geographically restricted, old conference study. It does not describe national or 2026 knowledge. Do not turn its questionnaire or older safety suggestions into operational medical/safety guidance. It reports no intervention trial.
- **Evidence strength:** Primary exploratory evidence.

## 2. Recent newspaper coverage and corroboration

### N1 — Odisha: outreach concerns reported in 2025

- **Author/date/type:** Hemant Kumar Rout, *The New Indian Express*, 26 May 2025; state news report.
- **Source:** [Lightning deaths rise as early warning apps fail to serve: Study](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/odisha/2025/May/26/lightning-deaths-rise-as-early-warning-apps-fail-to-serve-study).
- **Scope/finding:** Reports findings attributed to a Fakir Mohan University lightning monitoring awareness programme, describing difficulty getting warnings to exposed communities in Odisha.
- **Corroboration/limits:** The original underlying study, sampling method and case register were not recovered. Consequently, the article’s exposure/time-of-day percentages and app-failure conclusion are **not adopted as verified research results**. It offers regional reporting on a concern; it does not establish that Damini, SACHET or Mausam cause or fail to reduce deaths. It is not a substitute for a user survey or controlled evaluation.
- **Safe use:** Cite as recent local reporting that motivates investigating alert reach and comprehension. Keep source attribution explicit.

### N2 — Uttar Pradesh: a storm is not a lightning-only fatality event

- **Author/date/type:** TNN, *The Times of India*, updated 15 May 2026, 11:26 IST; event report concerning 13 May 2026.
- **Source:** [117 killed as storms lash UP with cyclone-like 130kmph winds](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/devastating-storm-across-up-claims-117-lives-damages-homes-livestock-across-up/articleshow/131102356.cms).
- **Scope/finding:** The article attributes a provisional **117 deaths** to the UP Relief Commissioner’s office: **113** from storms/collapsing structures and **four** from lightning.
- **Official cross-check:** [IMD’s report on 13 May 2026 thunderstorm activity](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/Forecast/marquee_data/Thunderstorm%20Report%20for%20the%20weather%20events%20of%2013.05.2026.pdf), dated 14 May, documents the event and primarily wind-related damage; p. 3, Table 2 records **130 km/h** at named stations in Bareilly and Prayagraj. This verifies event conditions, not the newspaper’s later exact casualty partition.
- **Limits:** Death totals changed as reports arrived. Do not label 117 as lightning deaths, a final certified total, or a forecast-accuracy statistic. A station gust is not a district-wide wind measurement.
- **Safe use:** Establishes why shelter guidance must consider wind and structure safety alongside lightning, with event and publication dates retained.

### N3 — Delhi-NCR: emergency weather alerts are already operational news

- **Author/date/type:** Express Web Desk, *The Indian Express*, published 30 May 2026, 17:44 IST; updated 17:59 IST; technology/weather news report.
- **Source:** [Delhi-NCR residents receive ‘Extremely Severe Alert’ on phones; first such after SACHET launch](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/delhi-ncr-extremely-severe-alert-phones-sachet-launch-10715893/).
- **Scope/finding:** Reports weather-warning pop-ups received by some Delhi-NCR residents, including Hindi information on thunderstorms, lightning and strong winds.
- **Official cross-check:** The Ministry of Communications independently confirms the **2 May 2026 launch of the Cell Broadcast System**, integrated with the CAP-based SACHET platform. [PIB release 2257499](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2257499&lang=1&reg=3).
- **Limits:** The newspaper body hedges the specific notification’s system linkage. Neither its “first such” headline nor geographic completeness of receipt was independently established. Newspaper reports of some recipients and a launch announcement cannot demonstrate universal delivery, comprehension or protective action.
- **Safe use:** Show the existing public-warning landscape and the importance of complementing official services. Do not claim that basic phone alerts are new to this proposal.

## 3. Implications for a distinctive, testable proposal

These are **design interpretations**, not measured outcomes of the proposed system:

| Evidence | Proposed response | What the pilot must establish |
|---|---|---|
| S1: response barriers include uncertainty, actions and impacts | Present one short action card with hazard, location, expiry, impact and uncertainty; link to the original warning | Comprehension of source, urgency, expiry and action; compare against the existing message |
| S2: established advisory beneficiaries report changing decisions | Fit alerts into a supervisor’s work pause, shelter and resumption workflow | Whether the workflow changes timely decisions; avoid extrapolating agricultural income effects |
| S3: local knowledge can vary by safety concept | Test shelter choices and warning interpretation with local participants before deployment | Item-level comprehension by language and role, including incorrect answers and nonresponses |
| N1: local reporting identifies possible outreach gaps | Include coordinators and non-app channels in discovery and drills | Receipt, understanding and action are separate endpoints; measure each |
| N2: wind and structural damage accompany lightning events | Require reviewed shelter records and multi-hazard instructions | Whether shelters are available, accessible and appropriate under the actual hazard combination |
| N3: public mobile warnings already exist | Build an action/coordination layer around authorized official information | Demonstrable benefit beyond existing services without modifying official warning meaning |

The [May 2026 official launch release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2257499&lang=1&reg=3) describes tower/cluster targeting, multilingual messages, 2G–5G support and read-aloud text on supported handsets. These features are prior art. The release is a government account of the system and its intended capabilities, not an independent delivery audit. Cell broadcast is also not an automatically available API for student projects: an authorized institutional integration remains a deployment dependency.

## 4. Claim controls for the proposal and pitch

- Preserve **population + sample + year + metric** whenever quoting survey percentages. Attribute all observations to their study.
- Do not promise a quantified reduction in deaths, a lives-saved count, model accuracy, village coverage, or economic return from these references. None evaluates this prototype.
- Do not present news reports, online newspaper polls or anecdotal interviews as representative survey evidence.
- Do not merge IMD multi-hazard event counts with NCRB lightning-only deaths. See the historical denominator-checked figures in [PROBLEM_EVIDENCE.md](PROBLEM_EVIDENCE.md).
- No contemporary representative national lightning-warning-comprehension survey was verified in this bounded review. This is an evidence gap, not proof that no such survey exists.
- For the project’s future study, record invitation/response counts, recruitment, language, role, region, survey date and exact item denominators. Separate a convenience usability test from a population estimate; explicitly report null results and failures. This is a proposed validation method, not completed research.
