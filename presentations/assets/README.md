## Forecast overlay

The PowerPoint adds native editable shapes for a possible cloud approach, future path and changing rain footprint. These overlays illustrate the intended product display. They are not generated by VAJRA inference and are not NASA rainfall data.

The visible caption must distinguish the observed backdrop from the illustrative overlay. A single satellite still does not establish movement, current conditions, surface rain or future skill. The daily VIIRS mosaic also does not demonstrate the proposed five-minute source-check cadence. INSAT sequences and other timestamped observations are separate training and serving inputs.
